This browser can’t talk to a serial port

Flashing and driving a board need the Web Serial API, which today means Chrome, Edge or Opera on desktop. Firefox has it only in Nightly behind a flag; Safari doesn’t implement it.

The Simulate tab works everywhere and needs no hardware.

1 · Flash the board

Puts examples/WebServoDemo on your Arduino. No toolchain, no library installs — it is already compiled.

Nano clones split between the two bootloaders and nothing on the port says which. If flashing fails to sync, try the other one.

Wire the servo before you drive it. Signal to A3, and power the servo from its own 4.8–6 V supply with a common ground — a servo’s stall current will brown out a USB-powered board mid-move.

Degrees, if you know them

Optional, and it is your figure rather than a measurement — this demo has no encoder, so it cannot check it.

“1000–2000 µs is 180°” is a convention, not a specification. The servo on this library’s bench rig does 0.0888 °/µs — that range spans 89.7°, half what everyone assumes. Measuring it needs an encoder, so the charts stay in microseconds.
